AA Rent A Car

A car rental website in four languages

Live
aarentacar.github.io/AA-RENT-CAR

AA Rent A Car needed a way for customers, including travellers who need an airport pickup, to see the cars and book without making a phone call.

The site works in English, Albanian, Macedonian and Turkish. Its booking form turns the customer's details into a ready-to-send WhatsApp message, so every request lands straight on the business's phone.

Open the live site

What I built

  • A language switcher for four languages, including Macedonian in Cyrillic
  • A booking form (name, phone, dates, car) that opens WhatsApp with the message already written
  • Fleet, reviews, FAQ, gallery and a map to the location
  • A mobile-first layout with a floating WhatsApp button

Made with

HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

Rapi Coiffure

A barbershop website with online booking

Almost finished
rapicoiffure.netlify.app

Rapi Coiffure's clients book in French or English. The site walks them through a short booking flow, one step at a time, and sends the request to the shop on WhatsApp.

Once the shop confirms the appointment, the client receives a payment link. Nobody pays for a slot that hasn't been confirmed, and the shop stays in control of its calendar.

Open the live site

What I built

  • French by default, with English one tap away
  • A five-step booking flow with progress, back and continue at every step
  • Booking requests sent to WhatsApp, with payment after the shop confirms
  • Services and prices, a gallery, opening hours and contact details

Made with

HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Hosted on Netlify.
